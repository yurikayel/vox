## Brand & Style

This design system synthesizes classical civic monumentalism with the kinetic, cryptographic vitality of peer-to-peer decentralized networks. It merges the architectural permanence of the ancient Athenian Agora—stoas, fluted colonnades, and deliberate deliberative space—with an emissive, cybernetic telemetry grid. 

The aesthetic operates on **Architectural Monolithicism meets Luminescent Cyber-Kineticism**. The interface deliberately eliminates claustrophobic, disposable rounded cards and generic gray dashboards in favor of continuous, unfragmented planar fields. The UI evokes the solemn gravitas of an immutable constitutional forum while maintaining the immediate responsiveness, real-time observability, and peer discovery of a distributed mesh.

The visual mood is profound, solemn, and electrifying: deep OLED obsidian stone beneath warm travertine inscriptions, punctured by the bioluminescent energy of emerald quorum states, cobalt protocol routing, and radiant energy gradients.

## Colors

The chromatic hierarchy pairs mineral stability with luminescent protocol energy on a pure obsidian-slate ground:

- **Obsidian & Deep Slate Ground (`#0d0f12` to `#111316`):** The foundational substrate. It absorbs eye strain, optimizes OLED displays, and establishes the atmospheric weight of a nocturnal marble hall.
- **Travertine & Warm Linen (`#f4eedd` / `#e2dcce`):** The epigraphic voice. Warm, tactile ivory tones replace stark, clinical white for major titles, constitutional prose, and reflective surfaces, delivering classical humanism.
- **Consensus Emerald (`#10b981` / `#34d399`):** Represents absolute finality, cryptographic ratification, peer health, and validated sovereign agency.
- **Attic Cobalt (`#3b82f6` / `#2563eb`):** Identifies peer discovery, cryptographic handshakes, telemetry streams, and transport routing.
- **Luminescent Aurora Gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10b981 0%, #06b6d4 50%, #3b82f6 100%)`):** Used strictly for high-order kinetic triggers, quorum seals, sovereign primary actions, and peer-to-peer presence beacons.
- **Structural Entablature (`#232830`):** Replaces container borders with rhythmic, monolithic architectural lines that divide the viewport into clean functional colonnades.

## Typography

The typographic system creates dynamic tension between classical epigraphy and precision engineering:

- **Epigraphic Monumentality (`Noto Serif`):** Reserved for display titles, civic propositions, and constitutional headings. Rendered in Travertine (`#f4eedd`). When applied in uppercase via `title-monument`, generous letter-spacing mirrors inscriptions chiseled into marble friezes.
- **High-Velocity Interface & Cryptographic Clarity (`Inter`):** Applied across all body reading, interface interactions, peer metrics, and telemetry data. 
- **Tabular Figures & Numeric Precision:** All data streams, voting tallies, cryptographic hashes, peer node IDs, and timestamps strictly enforce `font-feature-settings: "tnum" 1, "zero" 1` to ensure absolute optical stability without layout shifts during live protocol synchronization.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is not communicated through generic drop shadows or skeuomorphic bevels. Instead, this system uses **Lithic Stratification and Bioluminescent Projection**:

1. **The Basalt Bedrock (Z-0):** Unbroken `#0d0f12` OLED black. It serves as the primary canvas for all text and data.
2. **Monolithic Orthostates (Z-1):** Subtle surface shifts (`#111316` to `#171a1f`) framed by 1px entablature lines (`#232830`). Surfaces are planar extensions of the floor, not detached layers.
3. **Architectural Inset Lines:** Hovered panels or active sectors do not lift upwards; instead, their framing hairline transitions to Travertine at 15% opacity (`rgba(244, 238, 221, 0.15)`) or Consensus Emerald (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.3)`).
4. **Bioluminescent Emission (Z-2):** Interactive triggers, sovereign consensus seals, and peer discovery beacons project outward through pure light:
   - Primary Gradient Halo: `0 0 24px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.25), 0 0 48px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.15)`
   - Node Presence Pulse: `0 0 12px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.6)`
5. **Constitutional Modal Stelae (Z-3):** Dialogs appear as architectural stone slabs anchored over a deep backdrop blur (`rgba(13, 15, 18, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)`), crowned with a razor-thin 1px linear gradient header stroke (`#10b981` to `#3b82f6`).

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a disciplined **Precision Architectural Softness (`1`)**—a hybrid of classical squared ashlar masonry and refined micro-radii:

- **Structural Elements (4px / 0.25rem):** Action buttons, input surfaces, active panels, protocol monitors, and modal containers use precise 4px corners. This maintains an architectural, monolithic aesthetic without harsh unfinished edges.
- **P2P Rings & Presence Nodes (Full Pill / Circles):** Real-time node presence, consensus quorum rings, and connection indicators use pure circular forms (50% / `rounded-full`) to represent the living, organic mesh atop the stone substrate.
- **Chamfered Architectural Accents:** Ratified constitutional decrees and high-tier consensus seals may feature 45-degree corner chamfers (4px cutouts), referencing classical beveled pediments.
- Soft rounded blobs and playful organic shapes are strictly prohibited.

## Components

### Buttons & Sovereign Triggers
- **Primary Kinetic Action:** Finished with the signature Emerald-to-Cobalt gradient (`linear-gradient(135deg, #10b981 0%, #06b6d4 50%, #3b82f6 100%)`). Text is set in bold Obsidian (`#0d0f12`) in `Inter` with 4px border-radius. On hover, the button displays a radiant dual-glow (`0 0 20px rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.35), 0 0 20px rgba(59, 130, 246, 0.35)`) and micro-shifts 1px downward to communicate physical weight.
- **Secondary Travertine Inscription:** Solid background in Obsidian (`#111316`) framed by a 1px border in Linen (`rgba(244, 238, 221, 0.2)`), with Travertine text (`#f4eedd`). On hover, the border illuminates to solid `#f4eedd`.
- **Ghost Protocol Trigger:** Borderless; Travertine text (`#e2dcce`) with an active leading cobalt or emerald dot.

### The "No-Card" Planar Stelae
- In place of traditional rounded cards, grouped items exist as **Planar Stelae**: continuous horizontal bands delineated solely by an entablature hairline (`1px solid #232830`) at top and bottom.
- Backgrounds remain `#0d0f12` or subtle `#111316`. On hover, the entire section subtly shifts to `#171a1f` while the left boundary illuminates with a 2px vertical emerald or cobalt indicator bar.

### P2P Presence Rings & Quorum Badges
- **P2P Discovery Rings:** Concentric circular rings rendered with a 1.5px stroke flowing through the kinetic gradient (`#10b981` to `#3b82f6`). Active peers pulse through subtle CSS keyframe opacity adjustments.
- **Consensus Quorum Badges:** Compact pill enclosures (`rounded-full`) with a deep emerald tint (`rgba(16, 185, 129, 0.12)`), 1px emerald border, and Travertine typography flanked by a pulsing emerald status core.

### Inputs & Civic Registries
- Inputs sit directly on the planar surface without raised box elevation. They feature a `#111316` fill, 4px corner rounding, and a 1px hairline border in `#232830`.
- Text entry is set in `Inter` with tabular formatting.
- **Focus State:** The bottom border transforms into a vibrant 2px gradient line (`#10b981` to `#3b82f6`), accompanied by a soft glow across the base of the field.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes, Radios, Toggles)
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 16px squares (radii 3px) or circles with a 1.5px border in Travertine Linen (`#e2dcce` at 40% opacity). When checked, they fill with Consensus Emerald (`#10b981`), displaying an Obsidian (`#0d0f12`) glyph.
- **Matrix Toggles:** Track in Obsidian with a 1px border in `#232830`. Active tracks switch to the kinetic gradient (`#10b981` to `#3b82f6`) with an illuminated pure Travertine white thumb.

### Data Colonnades & Telemetry Graphs
- **Colonnade Lists:** Dense protocol records and quorum votes use alternate line stratification without outer boxes, separated by 1px dividers with tabular hash keys in Cobalt (`#3b82f6`) and validation counts in Emerald (`#10b981`).
- **Telemetry Sparklines:** Fine SVG path lines rendered with horizontal gradient fills flowing from historical consensus emerald into real-time cobalt leading edges.
